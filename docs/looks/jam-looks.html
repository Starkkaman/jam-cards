How switching works (proposal)

Each look is one Home Assistant theme (JamBoard, Jam Braun, Jam NERV, Jam LCARS, Jam Transit, Jam Olympia). The theme carries the look's colours, fonts, corner shapes and a look name the Jam cards read to switch their small decor (LCARS elbows, Braun toggles, NERV labels). A "Look" picker on Jam Control sets the theme for everyone at once; each person can still pick their own in their profile. Light/dark modes stay per look. The dashboards and kiosk layouts keep their structure in every look.
