2 · Group lights that stay readable

Stripes behind text made the names hard to read. Each option below keeps the words on one solid colour and shows the bulbs somewhere else.

Stove lights
Group · 3 bulbs

A · Label plate
Stripes fill the card; the name and number sit on black cut-paper labels, like a poster credit.

60%
B · Colour rail
Dark card, cream text. A stack of swatches down the left edge, one per bulb. Calmest; groups stand apart from single lights.
Stove lights
60%
C · Brightest bulb + chips
The card takes the brightest bulb's colour (not an average); a chip per bulb sits under the name.
Stove lights
Group · 3 bulbs
60%
D · Split
Text on a solid block at the left, your stripes on the right third, where no text goes.

Whichever you pick, single lights also get a check: names switch between black and cream text by how bright the colour is, and very pale or very saturated colours get the same label plate as A when contrast drops too low.
